How alignment works
  • Nothing changes until Apply. Cancel keeps the current diagram.
  • The reference feature keeps its place; each record moves so its chosen feature lines up with it.
  • Reversing flips a whole record with its labels and ribbons. Strands in the data (+/−) do not change.
  • The direction choice covers the reference and every chosen feature with a known direction. Skipped records and records without a usable candidate stay where they are.
  • Reciprocal best hit: the two proteins are each other's best match.
  • Automatic diagram fitting and zoom may move records on screen.
